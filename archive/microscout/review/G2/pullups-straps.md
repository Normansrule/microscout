# Pull-ups, pull-downs and strapping pins (G2)

Generated by `tools/sch/summaries.py` from the schematic sources. A resistor counts as a pull when one end is a
supply or GND and the other a signal. Dividers and filters also appear (noted).

## ESP32-S3 strapping pins

| Pin | Net | Circuit | Meaning at reset | Level at reset (design intent) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPIO0 | BOOT_N | R31 10 k to 3V3 + BOOT button to GND | 1 = SPI boot (normal); 0 with GPIO46 = 0 = download mode | High at reset unless BOOT is held. No capacitor on the pin (Espressif checklist). |
| GPIO3 | EXP_INT_N | R32 10 k to 3V3; open-drain sources only (2x TCA6408A INT, INA226 ALERT) | JTAG source select, read only if the STRAP_JTAG_SEL eFuse is burned | Never burn that eFuse. The open-drain sources cannot drive it high or low at reset except by pulling low if an interrupt is pending - harmless while the eFuse is not burned. |
| GPIO45 | LED_DIN_3V3 | R33 10 k to GND; drives only the SN74AHCT1G125 input (high impedance) | 0 = 3.3 V flash/PSRAM supply (required for N8R2) | Must be 0 at reset - held low by R33 and the module's weak pull-down. |
| GPIO46 | BUZZER | R60 100 k to GND at the Q3 gate | 0 required for download mode with GPIO0 = 0; also ROM log routing | Low at reset; FET off, buzzer silent. |

Other boot-sensitive lines:

- **DShot outputs GPIO2/21/38/47:** 100 k pull-downs (R34-R37) hold the lines low while the ESP32 resets, so the ESCs see no frames (DR-03). Bidirectional DShot idles high once running; whether AM32 accepts a low line at its own boot is a G6 check.
- **SPI chip selects:** IMU_CS_N (R52) and FLOW_CS_N (R56) pulled up so both sensors are deselected during boot.
- **EN (reset):** R30 10 k + C33 1 uF (Espressif RC). **PWR_KILL_N:** R14 100 k up holds the board on; GPIO48 or U23 pull it low to power off.
- **AT32F421 (each ESC MCU):** BOOT0 10 k to GND (boot from flash); NRST has 100 nF to GND and the chip's internal pull-up.
- **TCA6408A:** RESET pulled up (R53); all expander pins start as inputs, so the outside pulls set the safe state.
