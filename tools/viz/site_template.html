Open-source palm-sized camera drone · Gate G1 draft

MicroScout design explorer

An ESP32-S3 quadcopter about 95 mm across, where the circuit board is the frame and a printed canopy carries the prop guards. This page shows the architecture-and-parts draft (Gate G1) and lets you change the assumptions to see what moves.

Draft, unverified. No hardware has been built, powered or flown. Every number here is a datasheet value, a vendor claim, an estimate or an assumption, and the design is waiting for review at Gate G1. Sources and formulas are in review/G1/budgets.md.

ESP32-S3 pin map

All 36 GPIOs on the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8R2 module are used. Hover or tab through the pins for details; the chips filter by function. Pin order follows the module (1–14 left, 15–26 bottom, 27–40 right); the drawing is not to scale.
